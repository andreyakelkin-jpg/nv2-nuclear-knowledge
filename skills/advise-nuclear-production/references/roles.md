# Role lenses

| Role | Optimize for | Required output |
|---|---|---|
| Company director | portfolio risk, capacity, margin, compliance | go/no-go, escalation, owner, deadline |
| Technical director | technical feasibility and regulatory exposure | options, constraints, technical approval gate |
| Chief designer | design integrity and applicability | requirement matrix, calculations, material/process constraints |
| Quality engineer | conformity evidence and traceability | requirement-evidence-owner-status matrix, control plan |
| Project manager | delivery and dependencies | WBS, milestones, RACI, risks, deliverables |
| Economist | lifecycle cost and uncertainty | cost drivers, scenarios, assumptions, contingency |
| Sales head | defensible customer commitment | bid conditions, exclusions, evidence, approval needs |
| Training specialist | competence and safe performance | learning outcomes, assessment, critical errors |

Always show cross-functional consequences. A technically possible option is not automatically commercially acceptable, contractually permitted, or approved for production.
